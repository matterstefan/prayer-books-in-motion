# Prayer Books in Motion

**Prayer Books in Motion** is an experimental data-reuse and visualisation project devoted to fifteenth-century printed prayer books.

The project brings together openly accessible bibliographic and provenance data in order to explore the surviving copies, geographical distribution and historical movements of printed prayer books.

## Project aims

The project will:

- define a corpus of fifteenth-century printed prayer books;
- connect records from the *Gesamtkatalog der Wiegendrucke* (GW), the *Incunabula Short Title Catalogue* (ISTC) and *Material Evidence in Incunabula* (MEI);
- aggregate openly accessible data without creating new catalogue or provenance records;
- explore the relationships between editions, surviving copies, places and provenance events;
- develop an interactive web visualisation for scholarly exploration.

## Corpus

The initial corpus is based on:

- editions recorded under **Horae** in the GW;
- selected prayer books recorded under their own work titles in the GW.

The corpus is intended as a transparent and reproducible working corpus rather than a definitive scholarly classification of printed prayer books.

## Data sources

- [Gesamtkatalog der Wiegendrucke (GW)](https://www.gesamtkatalogderwiegendrucke.de/)
- [Incunabula Short Title Catalogue (ISTC)](https://data.cerl.org/istc/)
- [Material Evidence in Incunabula (MEI)](https://data.cerl.org/mei/)

The identifiers and terminology of the source catalogues will be retained wherever possible. No new provenance data will be produced.

## Status

The project is currently in an early development phase. Data structures, automated workflows and visualisation methods are being tested and documented in this repository.

## Context

The project is being developed in the context of COST Action CA23143, **Participation through Prayer in the Late Medieval and Early Modern World (PRAYTICIPATE)**, particularly Working Group 1, **Materialities of Prayer**.
